Attendance Dashboard Contribution

A contribution-based attendance web app centered on employee details, daily time summaries, and attendance status tracking.

Team Project Attendance Tracking Employee Dashboard Contribution Work

Functionality

What this type of web app does

  • Shows employee identity details together with role or department context.
  • Summarizes average working hours, check-in time, check-out time, and break time in one dashboard view.
  • Lists attendance entries by date with status indicators such as present or leave.
  • Supports quick review and small record actions through table-based controls.
